# personal_portfolio
Vaibhav Ramji is a senior in the Computer Science and Applied Engineering Academy at Hunterdon County Academies in Glen Gardner, New Jersey, where he has earned a 95.73 GPA through a rigorous college-preparatory curriculum, including AP Chemistry, AP Physics C, AP Calculus AB and BC, and AP Computer Science. Passionate about technology, engineering and developing unique solutions to emerging problems, Vaibhav interned at Techthought.org, where he built a Python script for automated news aggregation and social media posting, developed a GUI agent, and installed and configured Linux operating systems and virtual machines.

In addition to his academic achievements, Vaibhav is a dedicated student-athlete and member of the Voorhees High School boy’s tennis team. He is actively involved in numerous school organizations, serving as Vice President of the National Honor Society and President of the Voorhees Video Club, and participating in the Technology Student Association, Future Business Leaders of America, and Model UN, where he has developed leadership, teamwork and communication skills. He is also a member of the National Technical Honor Society. His accomplishments have earned him first place in MIT’s Zero Robotics competition, an eighth-place finish in the FBLA competition with qualification for its cybersecurity conference, and recognition as a Commended Scholar, AP Scholar, and College Board National Recognition Program Honoree.

Outside of school, Vaibhav volunteers as a youth tennis coach and has contributed over 75 leadership hours as a teacher’s assistant at the Hunterdon Art Museum summer camp, supporting young learners in his community. He also shares this enthusiasm for engineering through his personal tech blog and designed the Voorhees boys’ tennis team website. Through his commitment to academics, service and extracurricular involvement, Vaibhav strives to make a positive impact both inside and outside the classroom.
